# Setting Goals: My Health Goal

A single-file goal-setting activity for Grade 8 STEAM, Lesson 1: Whole Health. It replaces a Jamboard activity that no longer works, and follows on from the Whole Health Brainstorm in the same lesson.

Students pick one part of their health to work on, write a specific goal, say why it matters to them, list up to three steps, note how they will know it is working, plan for what might get in the way, name who can support them, and choose when they will check their progress. The app compiles their answers into a printable goal card they can print or copy into their portfolio.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
